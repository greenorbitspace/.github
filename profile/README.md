## **Green Orbit Space — GitHub Profile README**

### 🛰️ Welcome to Green Orbit Space Communications & PR

We help space organisations tell clearer, stronger, and more responsible stories. Whether you’re advancing launch systems, EO science, in-orbit services, or upstream innovation, we focus on communications that build trust, credibility, and long-term reputation.

### 📣 What We Do

* **Strategic communications & narrative development**
* **Press office & media relations** for the global space sector
* **Campaign delivery** across digital, broadcast, and industry press
* **Stakeholder engagement** including government, investors, and partners
* **Crisis & integrity comms** rooted in transparency

### 🌍 Why It Matters

Space is entering a new era—crowded, commercial, and politically complex. Clear communication is no longer optional. We champion storytelling that strengthens sustainability, accountability, and public understanding.

### 🛠 Current Work

* Open-source tools for space comms teams
* Message frameworks supporting responsible space operations
* Improving public literacy around space sustainability

### 📡 Connect

Let’s tell the story of a more sustainable and accessible space future.
